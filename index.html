About Walmet Agrihub

Who We Are

Walmet Agrihub is a Kenyan private-sector enterprise dedicated to advancing regenerative agriculture, climate adaptation, resilient food systems and sustainable rural development. We bring together technical expertise, practical experience and a partnership-driven approach to deliver solutions that create lasting impact.

Learn More

Our Vision

Resilient and regenerative food systems that enable productive landscapes, thriving communities and sustainable livelihoods.

Our Mission

To provide evidence-informed agricultural, environmental and development solutions that strengthen food systems, restore landscapes, improve rural livelihoods and build climate resilience.
